Global M&A activity increases in 9M’26, reports WTW

Global mergers and acquisitions (M&A) activity increased in the first nine months of 2026, with higher-value transactions contributing to a rise in overall deal value, according to WTW, the global professional services company providing risk, insurance, investment and consulting services. 4. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Hannover Re targets $200m seventh Acorn Re parametric US earthquake cat bond

Global reinsurance firm Hannover Re is bringing the seventh catastrophe bond offering in the annual parametric US earthquake risk series of deals, with an initial $200 million target for an Acorn Re Ltd. Just like the six Acorn Re cat bond deals to come before, this new Acorn Re 2026-1 issuance will also provide protection to cover other Hannover Re reinsureds, that have exposure within the parametric earthquake boxes should a major quake event occur. As seen with all six of the previous Acorn Re parametric catastrophe bonds , this new seventh issuance from the special purpose insurer is being brought to market by Hannover Re, acting as the ceding reinsurance company, sitting in front of and providing protection to a single named ceding insurer, Oak Tree Assurance Ltd.
