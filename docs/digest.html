1. 💰 Inversión $1800M

Summer storms across the Canadian Prairies caused over $1.8bn in insured losses: CatIQ

Ten extreme weather events impacted the Canadian Prairies this summer, causing widespread damage and over $1.8 billion in insured losses, according to initial estimates from CatIQ. Between June 9th and 10th over $900 million in insured losses in Saskatchewan and Manitoba were caused by a series of severe storms that brought tornadoes, hail, damaging winds, torrential rain and flash flooding to communities across both provinces. Between June 20th and 21st, prolonged rainfall caused extensive flooding in Edmonton and the surrounding area, which caused over $160 million in insured losses, While between the 26th and 30th, all three provinces were impacted by a slow-moving storm which moved across the Prairies, causing extensive flooding with Canmore and Kananaskis County, Alberta declaring local states of emergency.

4. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Strengthening Hurricane Polo brings Mexico catastrophe bond into focus

Hurricane Polo is rapidly intensifying off the southwestern coast of Mexico and the strengthening trend is expected to continue, while the track remains uncertain at this time, which has brought the $175 million IBRD CAR Mexico 2024 (Pacific) parametric catastrophe bond into focus this week. Any deviation closer to the coastline of Mexico could take hurricane Polo very near to the IBRD Mexico 2024 catastrophe bond’s parametric trigger box arrangement, which runs up the coastline. However, the current location of hurricane Polo is close to the Mexican coastline and any wobble in the path as the storm moves forwards could bring it much closer to the coastline and the parametric box of the catastrophe bond, which runs just off the shore.
