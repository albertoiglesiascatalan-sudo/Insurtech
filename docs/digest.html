2. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Verisk says insured losses from Kumamoto Earthquake in Japan could hit $2.1bn

According to initial estimates by the Catastrophe and Risk Solutions group at Verisk, a data analytics and technology provider to the global re/insurance industry, the 6.8 magnitude earthquake that struck near Kumamoto City on Japan’s Kyushu Island on 28 July will result in insured losses of between JPY 220-340 billion, approximately USD 1.4-2.1 billion. This estimate accounts predominantly for insured property losses from ground shaking and liquefaction and is before any recoveries under Japan’s earthquake insurance program. According to initial reports by Aon and Howden Re , the July earthquake is expected to cause relatively lower losses than the 2016 Kumamoto earthquakes, which caused $53 billion in economic and $7.7 billion in insured losses.

💡 Impacto directo en exposición, pricing y reservas del sector.

Allianz backs Dili

Allianz participated in a $15 million Series A for Dili , an AI-powered compliance platform serving energy, infrastructure, construction and manufacturing projects. The financing brings Dili’s total funding to $21.7 million. Founded in 2023 by Anand Chaturvedi and Brian Fernandez, New York-based Dili automates prevailing-wage monitoring, apprenticeship compliance, certified-payroll reviews and audit reporting.
