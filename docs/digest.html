2. ⚖️ Regulación

Authorities complete 2026 review of payments Memorandum of Understanding

The Bank of England (the Bank), Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) and Payment Systems Regulator (PSR) have completed their annual review of the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on payments. The review found that cooperation and coordination remain effective, with respondents welcoming tangible progress in how the Authorities work together. Following the review, the Authorities have concluded that no changes to the MoU are needed at this time.

3. ⚖️ Regulación

California Department of Insurance completes sustainable insurance roadmap and introduces new insurability framework

The California Department of Insurance (CDI), the government agency responsible for regulating insurers and overseeing consumer protection in California’s insurance market, has completed its four-year Sustainable Insurance Roadmap and introduced a new framework designed to address the challenges of maintaining insurance coverage in areas exposed to increasing climate-related risks. The announcement was accompanied by the publication of Preserving Insurability, a report examining the outcomes of the initiative and setting out the principles behind the newly established California Framework for Insurable Communities. According to CDI, the Sustainable Insurance Roadmap was introduced in 2022 as the first programme of its kind internationally, seeking to examine how insurance regulation could respond to environmental threats while supporting market stability and policyholder protection.

ILS and alternative capital expanding footprint into DUAE market: AM Best

In a new commentary, rating agency AM Best has highlighted how alternative capital and insurance-linked securities (ILS) are increasingly entering the delegated underwriting authority enterprise (DUAE) space, with DUAEs utilising sidecars, catastrophe bonds, and collateralized reinsurance to secure capacity when insurers step back. In its latest market segment outlook, AM Best has maintained its stable outlook for the DUAE segment, with the agency citing an anticipated moderation in premium growth amid more disciplined expansion efforts. Private equity capital, for example, continues to flow into the segment, driving mergers and acquisitions, where smaller and less-differentiated players are likely to become acquisition targets,” AM Best said.
