Hanwha Life plans $664 million Acuon acquisition

Hanwha Life and private equity firm Centroid Investment Partners are set to acquire Acuon Capital and Acuon Savings Bank in a package deal valued at nearly 900 billion won ($664 million), according to The Korea Economic Daily. Hanwha Life was selected as the preferred bidder approximately three months ago, beating Meritz Securities. The transaction involves acquiring a 96.06% stake in Acuon Capital from EQT.
