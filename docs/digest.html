MNK Group acquires Danish non-life insurer ETU Forsikring

MNK Group, the independent international insurance group, has completed the acquisition of 100% of Danish insurer ETU Forsikring A/S, following approval from the Danish Financial Supervisory Authority (Finanstilsynet). According to the announcement, MNK Group’s main focus is to strengthen ETU as a Danish insurer. Established in 2006, ETU is a Danish non-life insurer and is headquartered in Rødekro, Southern Jutland.

3. 💰 Inversión $7M

HelmGuard raises $7.3 million

AI-powered security and compliance platform HelmGuard AI has raised $7.3 million in seed funding. HelmGuard uses AI agents to collect and assess risk information from source systems, automate third-party risk reviews and monitor the behavior of other AI agents. HelmGuard has offices in London, New York and San Francisco and was co-founded by former Palantir executive John Daley.

Hurricane Lowell Estimated Damage to Hawaiian Islands Between $.5B and $1B: Cotality

Insured losses from Hurricane Lowell are estimated at $0.5 billion to $1.0 billion, with most of the damage expected on Kauai, according to insight by Cotality. Hurricane Lowell passed just west of Niihau overnight as a Category 2 hurricane, bringing hurricane-force gusts, flooding rain, and destructive surf to Kauai County in the closest approach by a Category 2 or stronger hurricane to Kauai since Iniki in 1992, the data insight firm said. According to the Central Pacific Hurricane Center, Lowell’s center was located about 90 miles west of Lihue and 190 miles west-northwest of Honolulu at 11 p.m.
