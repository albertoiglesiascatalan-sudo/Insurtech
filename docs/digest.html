1. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Munich Re teams with Wawanesa & WWF-Canada to launch parametric river flood solution

Reinsurance firm Munich Re has partnered with Wawanesa and World Wildlife Fund Canada (WWF-Canada), to develop and launch a parametric river flood insurance solution that will deliver rapid funding to safeguard nature-based restoration across the Wolastoq (Saint John River) watershed. Under the parametric structure, Wawanesa funding will be triggered by independently measured river levels, and if predetermined river conditions are met, Wawanesa will provide up to $1 million in funding each year, capped at a maximum of $3 million over a three-year period, to WWF-Canada. The parametric arrangement was developed with support from Munich Re Canada, alongside climate-peril expertise from reinsurance broker Marsh Re.

Radian reports 106% revenue increase for Q3’26 as net premiums rise following Inigo deal

Radian Group Inc., a US financial services company operating in mortgage and specialty insurance, has reported a total revenue hike of 106% to $624 million for the third quarter of 2026, as net premiums earned rose to $548 million, of which 57%, or $311 million, came from the specialty segment following the firm’s acquisition of Inigo. Total revenue more than doubled year-over-year with the addition of Inigo, and the strategic rationale for the acquisition is already showing up in our results. For the quarter, Radian reported a net income from continuing operations of $172 million, compared to $153 million in Q3’25.

Jewelers Mutual acquires IFS Inforsure

Jewelers Mutual acquired IFS Inforsure, a Pasadena, California-based provider of insured shipping solutions for jewelry, watches and other high-value items.
