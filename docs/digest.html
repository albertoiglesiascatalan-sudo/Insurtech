3. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

CatIQ finalises loss estimate for Hurricane Debby floods in Ontario & Quebec at C$2.80bn

CatIQ, the Toronto-based independent catastrophe insurance data provider, has published a final insurance industry loss estimate of CAD $2.80 billion from the severe flooding generated by the remnants of Hurricane Debby that impacted Ontario and Quebec between August 9th and 10th, 2024. Moreover, CatIQ’s final loss estimate is also down from its fourth loss estimate of $2.817 billion, which was issued six months after the event. However, this final estimate is still an increase from CatIQ’s third loss estimate of $2.76 billion, which was issued 90 days after the event took place.

5. 💰 Inversión

Severe weather drives insured losses past $840m in Manitoba and Saskatchewan: CatIQ

Severe weather losses in Manitoba and Saskatchewan – neighbouring provinces located in Western and Central Canada – have climbed above $840 million, according to the latest estimate from Catastrophe Indices and Quantification Inc. Since 2021, Manitoba and Saskatchewan have experienced 30 catastrophic weather events resulting in over $2.5 billion in insured losses. So far in 2026, five of Canada’s 11 catastrophic weather events have affected Manitoba and Saskatchewan, including a dust storm with damaging winds that caused $40 million in insured losses.
