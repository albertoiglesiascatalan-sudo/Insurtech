1. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Munich Re teams with Wawanesa & WWF-Canada to launch parametric river flood solution

Reinsurance firm Munich Re has partnered with Wawanesa and World Wildlife Fund Canada (WWF-Canada), to develop and launch a parametric river flood insurance solution that will deliver rapid funding to safeguard nature-based restoration across the Wolastoq (Saint John River) watershed. Under the parametric structure, Wawanesa funding will be triggered by independently measured river levels, and if predetermined river conditions are met, Wawanesa will provide up to $1 million in funding each year, capped at a maximum of $3 million over a three-year period, to WWF-Canada. The parametric arrangement was developed with support from Munich Re Canada, alongside climate-peril expertise from reinsurance broker Marsh Re.

2. 💰 Inversión $21M

Nebuly raises $21 million to measure AI returns

AI analytics startup Nebuly raised a $21 million Series A led by Planven, with participation from Exor Ventures, Tenacity Capital, TGVG Capital, Fastweb+Vodafone via Founders Factory, and returning investors and angels. Based in Turin and New York, Nebuly helps companies measure the business value of AI assistants used by employees and customers. Nebuly connects to existing AI tools, including custom agents and assistants based on Claude, ChatGPT, and Microsoft Copilot, and can run on-premises or in a customer’s private cloud.

3. 👔 Cambio de Liderazgo

ANZ CEO Matos Warns of Global Jobs Crisis Without AI Rules

Chief Executive Officer Nuno Matos has called for global regulation to manage the impact of artificial intelligence on the labor market, which he warned could trigger an international crisis. Should large-scale job cuts of up to 50% emerge, “we’ll have a crisis and it’s not going to be in Australia, it’s going to be a global crisis,” Matos said Thursday during a parliamentary hearing in Canberra. “I hope that obviously the world and the administrations are capable of cooperating and ensuring that this technology does not create a social problem at a global level,” Matos said.

4. 🤝 M&A

Pinion receives approval to acquire Catlin Specialty

Pinion US Holdings has received change-of-control approval from the Oklahoma Department of Insurance to complete its acquisition of Catlin Specialty Insurance Company, a US excess and surplus lines insurer. The approval provides the regulated infrastructure for Pinion’s planned US underwriting operations, which will focus on providing capacity to specialty MGAs. Pinion launched in February 2026 with a commitment of up to $180 million from asset manager Barings.
