1. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Munich Re teams with Wawanesa & WWF-Canada to launch parametric river flood solution

Reinsurance firm Munich Re has partnered with Wawanesa and World Wildlife Fund Canada (WWF-Canada), to develop and launch a parametric river flood insurance solution that will deliver rapid funding to safeguard nature-based restoration across the Wolastoq (Saint John River) watershed. Under the parametric structure, Wawanesa funding will be triggered by independently measured river levels, and if predetermined river conditions are met, Wawanesa will provide up to $1 million in funding each year, capped at a maximum of $3 million over a three-year period, to WWF-Canada. The parametric arrangement was developed with support from Munich Re Canada, alongside climate-peril expertise from reinsurance broker Marsh Re.

5. ⚖️ Regulación

Peer Review of Türkiye

The FSB’s 2025 thematic peer review on the implementation of its global regulatory framework for crypto-asset activities showed that Türkiye had finalised a regulatory framework for crypto assets, although not for stablecoins. The FSB recommends further steps to reinforce Türkiye’s regulatory framework and improve alignment with the FSB Global Regulatory Framework for Crypto-asset Activities , focusing in particular on operationalising a framework for financial stability monitoring, establishing a dedicated regime for stablecoins, strengthening supervision and enforcement, and promoting effective cross-border cooperation and information sharing. Turkish authorities have made meaningful progress in enhancing the regulation, supervision and oversight of crypto-asset markets and activities in recent years.
