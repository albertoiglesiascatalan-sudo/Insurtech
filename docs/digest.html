ADB aims to expand catastrophe bond usage across Asian regions: Kessler

The Asian Development Bank (ADB) is actively promoting catastrophe bonds for sovereign disaster risk financing across the region, but Thomas Kessler noted last week that this will require cooperation across governments, research and modelling institutions, insurers and the ILS investor base. Speaking in Bermuda at the Convergence 2026 conference last week, Thomas Kessler, Principal Disaster Risk Insurance & Finance Specialist at the Asian Development Bank (ADB) explained that replicating its first successful catastrophe bond issuances takes time and the engagement of insurance-linked securities market participants. Corgi appoints James Kullman as Head of Underwriting, North America

Corgi Insurance, an AI-native insurance carrier built for high-growth companies, has announced the appointment of James Kullman as Head of Underwriting, North America. In his new role, Kullman will lead efforts to advance commercial underwriting across North America, leveraging AI to improve risk selection, develop underwriting guidelines, strengthen portfolio management and support regulatory compliance. He joins Corgi from Chubb, where he served as Assistant Vice President and Pacific North Region Underwriting Manager for the North American Small and Lower Middle Market.

3. 💰 Inversión

Jamaica’s parametric cat bond payout funded electricity restoration: Chief Fiscal Economist, Francis

After the devastating impacts of hurricane Melissa triggered a full $150 million payout of its IBRD CAR Jamaica 2024 parametric catastrophe bond, the rapid funding from this insurance product helped the country recover its electricity network faster than would have otherwise been possible, the government’s Chief Fiscal Economist explained last week. Speaking at the ILS Bermuda Convergence 2026 conference on the island last week, Terron Francis, Chief Fiscal Economist in the Ministry of Finance & the Public Service, Government of Jamaica highlighted the critical role of risk transfer and insurance risk capital in helping the Caribbean island nation to recover after the impactful storm. Francis said that the parametric catastrophe bond payout arrived within 30 days, supporting response and recovery for Jamaica, without forcing the government into emergency borrowing or substantial programme-budget reallocations.

HCI’s reinsurance tower and token affected after claims from Hurricane Isaias hit

HCI Group, Inc., a Tampa-based insurance holding company with operations in homeowners insurance, information technology services, real estate, and reinsurance, has confirmed that only one of its three reinsurance towers has been impacted after receiving claims from Hurricane Isaias. The firm said, “Anything above this number will be subject to the company’s reinsurance agreements.” Additionally, taking a look at HCI’s reinsurance tokens, only Token B is expected to be impacted by Hurricane Isaias, which remains profitable despite this hit. Former chief executive banned after misconduct led to £4m losses in high-risk investments

The FCA has banned Jeffrey Dawkins, the former chief executive of Vector Wealth Ltd, after he dishonestly acted as a front for a disqualified director using the company to sell high-risk bonds. Therese Chambers, executive director of enforcement and market oversight at the FCA, said, 'Jeffrey Dawkins acted as a front man for a banned director, helping high-risk investments look safer than they were. Mini-bonds and similar investments are very high risk, and people should be extremely cautious.' The FCA has warned about the risks of investing in mini-bonds and loan notes.
