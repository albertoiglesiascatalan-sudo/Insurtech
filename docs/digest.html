1. 🤝 M&A

MNK Group acquires Danish non-life insurer ETU Forsikring

MNK Group, the independent international insurance group, has completed the acquisition of 100% of Danish insurer ETU Forsikring A/S, following approval from the Danish Financial Supervisory Authority (Finanstilsynet). According to the announcement, MNK Group’s main focus is to strengthen ETU as a Danish insurer. Established in 2006, ETU is a Danish non-life insurer and is headquartered in Rødekro, Southern Jutland.

2. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Swiss Re highlights growing catastrophe protection gap as nat cat losses rise 5-7% annually

Reinsurance giant Swiss Re has highlighted how growing natural catastrophe risk remains a significant driver of reinsurance demand, as insured nat cat losses continue to follow a 5–7% annual growth rate, driven by increasing exposures, rising asset values and changing hazard patterns. Crucially, this rise in societal exposure underscores an expanding catastrophe protection gap, as well as a growing need for broader risk transfer solutions. At the 68th annual meeting of the reinsurance industry in Monte Carlo, Swiss Re shared that its modelling indicates that insured losses could reach around US $320 billion in a 2026 peak loss scenario, which clearly demonstrates the value of reinsurance protection against low-frequency, high-severity events.

💡 Impacto directo en exposición, pricing y reservas del sector.

3. 👔 Cambio de Liderazgo

Huxley to succeed Besemer as FOIL CEO

The Forum of Insurance Lawyers (FOIL), a professional UK membership organisation established in 1992 that represents lawyers defending insurance-related claims, has a ppointed Mark Huxley as its new Chief Executive Officer, effective October 1, 2026. Huxley, a renowned insurance professional, will be succeeding Laurence Besemer, who is moving on to pursue other ventures and consultancy projects within the insurance industry. According to the announcement, Besemer will stay at FOIL until the end of the calendar year to ensure a smooth handover to his successor.

4. 💰 Inversión $7M

HelmGuard raises $7.3 million

AI-powered security and compliance platform HelmGuard AI has raised $7.3 million in seed funding. HelmGuard uses AI agents to collect and assess risk information from source systems, automate third-party risk reviews and monitor the behavior of other AI agents. HelmGuard has offices in London, New York and San Francisco and was co-founded by former Palantir executive John Daley.

5. ⚖️ Regulación

Upper Tribunal upholds Crispin Odey ban

Crispin Odey’s ban from the financial services industry has been upheld by the Upper Tribunal, which found he lacked integrity. The Tribunal considered Mr Odey’s attempted justifications for removing the ExCos to be no more than a smokescreen. Alongside the allegations arising directly from his dismissal of the ExCo's, the Tribunal upheld the allegations that Mr Odey’s dealings with OAM, its clients, its investors and the FCA lacked candour.
