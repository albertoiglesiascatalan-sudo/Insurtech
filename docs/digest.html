2. 🤝 M&A

Frequent acquirers better placed to achieve insurance M&A goals: Oxbow Partners and InsurTech NY

Insurance companies with established acquisition programmes may be more successful at securing the anticipated benefits of mergers and acquisitions (M&A), according to a joint study by Oxbow Partners, a specialist insurance management consultancy, and InsurTech NY, an organisation supporting connections between insurers, technology businesses and investors. Oxbow Partners and InsurTech NY assessed 333 publicly listed insurance carriers and gathered perspectives from industry executives and transaction specialists with experience of significant insurance deals. Oxbow Partners and InsurTech NY suggest that decisions concerning how businesses will operate together should form part of the initial assessment of a potential acquisition.

5. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Insurance leaders call for collective approach to climate resilience at COP31

At COP31 in Türkiye, the Global Sustainable Insurance Summit examined how the insurance sector can help reduce climate risks, strengthen economic resilience and finance the sustainable transition. Organised by the Insurance Association of Türkiye (TSB) and the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) at the COP31 Insurance House, the Summit brought together executives and specialists from across the re/insurance and resilience sectors. TSB Secretary General Özgür Obalı opened the Summit by stressing that climate resilience is a collective challenge requiring cooperation between the public and private sectors.
