Hannover Re raises Acorn Re parametric cat bond target size again, to $450m

Hannover Re has for a second time increased the target size of what will be the seventh catastrophe bond offering in the annual Acorn Re parametric US earthquake series of deals, now seeking $450 million of limit from the Acorn Re Ltd. Hannover Re returned to market with this new and now seventh Acorn Re parametric catastrophe bond earlier this month , initially seeking $200 million of risk transfer and reinsurance limit for the single named ceding insurer, Oak Tree Assurance Ltd. Now, sources have told us the target size has been further increased, with $450 million of parametric earthquake reinsurance limit now being sought.

2. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

ADB aims to expand catastrophe bond usage across Asian regions: Kessler

The Asian Development Bank (ADB) is actively promoting catastrophe bonds for sovereign disaster risk financing across the region, but Thomas Kessler noted last week that this will require cooperation across governments, research and modelling institutions, insurers and the ILS investor base. Speaking in Bermuda at the Convergence 2026 conference last week, Thomas Kessler, Principal Disaster Risk Insurance & Finance Specialist at the Asian Development Bank (ADB) explained that replicating its first successful catastrophe bond issuances takes time and the engagement of insurance-linked securities market participants. As we reported back in April 2026 , the Asian Development Bank’s (ADB) first catastrophe bonds delivered $160 million of capital market-backed parametric earthquake and extreme precipitation disaster risk financing, evenly split between the Kyrgyz Republic and Tajikistan .

3. ⚖️ Regulación

Mexico gets its first World Bank Cat DDO, to complement cat bonds and parametric insurance

The Government of Mexico has received its first World Bank Catastrophe Deferred Drawdown Option (Cat DDO), which will help the country prepare better and respond more quickly to natural disasters, while also complementing both of its IBRD CAR Mexico 2024 and IBRD CAR Mexico 2024 (Pacific) catastrophe bonds and parametric insurance protection. The World Bank Group has approved US $500 million in financing, marking the first time Mexico has accessed a Catastrophe Deferred Drawdown Option (Cat DDO). We are proud to support the Government of Mexico in this first Cat DDO,” commented Juan Pablo Uribe, World Bank Director for Mexico, Central America, and the Dominican Republic.

4. 💰 Inversión

Jamaica’s parametric cat bond payout funded electricity restoration: Chief Fiscal Economist, Francis

After the devastating impacts of hurricane Melissa triggered a full $150 million payout of its IBRD CAR Jamaica 2024 parametric catastrophe bond, the rapid funding from this insurance product helped the country recover its electricity network faster than would have otherwise been possible, the government’s Chief Fiscal Economist explained last week. Speaking at the ILS Bermuda Convergence 2026 conference on the island last week, Terron Francis, Chief Fiscal Economist in the Ministry of Finance & the Public Service, Government of Jamaica highlighted the critical role of risk transfer and insurance risk capital in helping the Caribbean island nation to recover after the impactful storm. Francis said that the parametric catastrophe bond payout arrived within 30 days, supporting response and recovery for Jamaica, without forcing the government into emergency borrowing or substantial programme-budget reallocations.

Exclusive: Slingshot Raises $27M In Seed Funding To Build An Insurer For Those Small Policies Sold At Checkout

Slingshot Insurance , an AI-native embedded insurance startup, is emerging from stealth with $27 million in seed funding, it tells Crunchbase News exclusively. Slingshot, which is led by first-time founder and CEO Ethan Genteman , focuses on small insurance policies that consumers can buy as part of another purchase, such as coverage when booking a flight. The startup’s software helps businesses offer those policies by connecting them with insurers that back the coverage.
