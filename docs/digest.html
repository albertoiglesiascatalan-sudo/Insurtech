Former Apple Engineers’ Physical AI Startup Lyte Raises $165M At $1.6B Valuation

Lyte , a physical AI startup building sensing and perception technology for robots, has raised $165 million in Series C funding at a $1.6 billion post-money valuation. The Sunnyvale, California-based company was founded in 2021 by Alexander Shpunt , Arman Hajati and Yuval Gerson — a trio of former Apple engineers who worked on the iPhone giant’s advanced sensing and perception technologies. Shpunt had also previously co-founded PrimeSense , a startup whose 3D-sensing technology powered Microsoft Kinect before Apple acquired the company in 2013.
