Hanwha Life plans $664 million Acuon acquisition

Hanwha Life and private equity firm Centroid Investment Partners are set to acquire Acuon Capital and Acuon Savings Bank in a package deal valued at nearly 900 billion won ($664 million), according to The Korea Economic Daily. Hanwha Life was selected as the preferred bidder approximately three months ago, beating Meritz Securities. The transaction involves acquiring a 96.06% stake in Acuon Capital from EQT.

4. 🤝 M&A

SIAA acquires Canopy Specialty Insurance

SIAA has acquired Canopy Specialty Insurance, a specialty commercial property and casualty MGA operating in the US and UK. The acquisition adds specialty underwriting and product development capabilities alongside SIAA’s agency alliance, retail business Sequel Insurance Agencies and wholesale operation Xchange. Canopy develops and underwrites niche commercial insurance products for small and midsized businesses, including contractors’ liability, excess casualty and legal indemnity programs.
