Travelers remains open to M&A following strong Q2

During the company’s July 17 earnings call, Chairman and CEO Alan Schnitzer said Travelers remains interested in strategic acquisitions and would be able to finance an attractive opportunity. 4. 👔 Cambio de Liderazgo

People: Bowie Named CEO of Praxis Reciprocal Exchange

Tampa-based Praxis Reciprocal Exchange, approved to do business in Florida in September 2025, has named Ross Bowie chief executive officer, the company announced. As CEO, Bowie will lead underwriting, distribution, and strategy across the Praxis operating companies, working with President and CFO Jennifer Gravelle and the leadership team to build on the organization’s first-year momentum and grow the exchange and MGA, the company noted. Bowie comes to Praxis after two years as president, chief risk officer, then CEO of Private Client Select Insurance Services, a joint venture between AIG and Stone Point Capital that focuses on high-net-worth individuals.

You don't need to pay to claim: FCA launches nationwide car finance campaign

Millions of car finance customers who may be owed compensation can get help making a complaint for free, as the FCA launches a national advertising campaign. Our free tools are there to help people feel claim confident – so they can get any money owed back without it costing them a penny.' Many car finance customers are already acting – 59% have made or are considering a claim – but a significant group (23%) say they are unsure of their options. Research by the FCA found that 27% of car finance customers lack confidence to make a complaint without using a claims management company (CMC) or law firm, despite free tools from the FCA being available.
