3. 🤝 M&A

Travelers remains open to M&A following strong Q2

During the company’s July 17 earnings call, Chairman and CEO Alan Schnitzer said Travelers remains interested in strategic acquisitions and would be able to finance an attractive opportunity. Travelers reported $2.2 billion in second-quarter core income, or $10.04 per diluted share, as underwriting gains and higher investment income contributed to a core return on equity of 24.9%. Travelers’ most recent acquisition was cyber insurer Corvus Insurance, purchased for approximately $435 million in November 2023.

FCA and Bank appoint members to their Transaction and Post-trade Reporting Harmonisation Taskforce

The FCA and Bank of England (Bank) have appointed members to their Transaction and Post-trade Reporting Harmonisation Taskforce. Exclusive: Co-Founded By Former Whoop CTO, Throne Science Raises $10M To Track Gut Health From The Toilet

Throne Science , a startup that makes an AI-powered toilet camera to track gut health and hydration, has raised $10 million in a Series A round led by Will Ventures , it told Crunchbase News exclusively. The startup was founded in 2023 by CEO Scott Hickle , who previously worked for nearly a decade in software product management at various companies, and former Whoop co-founder and CTO John Capodilupo , for whom the company’s mission is also a bit of a personal one as he has ulcerative colitis. “We started Throne because we believe it’s inevitable that people will one day measure their health from their waste,” Hickle told Crunchbase News.
