Strengthening Hurricane Polo brings Mexico catastrophe bond into focus

Hurricane Polo is rapidly intensifying off the southwestern coast of Mexico and the strengthening trend is expected to continue, while the track remains uncertain at this time, which has brought the $175 million IBRD CAR Mexico 2024 (Pacific) parametric catastrophe bond into focus this week. Any deviation closer to the coastline of Mexico could take hurricane Polo very near to the IBRD Mexico 2024 catastrophe bond’s parametric trigger box arrangement, which runs up the coastline. However, the current location of hurricane Polo is close to the Mexican coastline and any wobble in the path as the storm moves forwards could bring it much closer to the coastline and the parametric box of the catastrophe bond, which runs just off the shore.

5. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

EU Touts Climate Insurance Pact to Ease Catastrophe Losses

The European Union will set up a “climate insurance alliance” to help pay for losses as global warming increases the frequency and intensity of droughts, floods and wildfires. European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen announced the initiative as part of her state of the union address Wednesday, saying that only a quarter of catastrophe losses are privately insured, and that the costs therefore fall on national budgets. A climate resilience framework, due next month, will map 100 of the most vulnerable regions in the bloc, she added.
