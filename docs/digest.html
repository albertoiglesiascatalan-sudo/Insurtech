Global M&A activity increases in 9M’26, reports WTW

Global mergers and acquisitions (M&A) activity increased in the first nine months of 2026, with higher-value transactions contributing to a rise in overall deal value, according to WTW, the global professional services company providing risk, insurance, investment and consulting services. WTW reported that activity accelerated during the third quarter, with nine mega deals completed compared with three in the previous quarter. Activity among deals valued between USD $1 billion and USD $10 billion was more subdued.

4. 🤝 M&A

SIAA acquires Canopy Specialty Insurance

SIAA has acquired Canopy Specialty Insurance, a specialty commercial property and casualty MGA operating in the US and UK. The acquisition adds specialty underwriting and product development capabilities alongside SIAA’s agency alliance, retail business Sequel Insurance Agencies and wholesale operation Xchange. Canopy develops and underwrites niche commercial insurance products for small and midsized businesses, including contractors’ liability, excess casualty and legal indemnity programs.

5. 💰 Inversión $37M

Meanwhile raises $37.5 million

Bermuda-based Bitcoin life insurer Meanwhile has raised $37.5 million from existing investors, bringing its total funding to more than $180 million. Earlier this year, Meanwhile launched BTC Life 1-Pay, a single-premium whole life policy for high-net-worth clients outside the US. Meanwhile has signed 15 brokers serving wealthy families, including Lioner and Apeiron Group, with distribution spanning Singapore, Hong Kong, the UAE, and Switzerland.
