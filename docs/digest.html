EU Commission proposes climate resilience framework, highlights cat bonds & reinsurance

The European Commission has called for a new climate resilience framework to strengthen Europe’s ability to anticipate, prepare for, and manage climate risks. “The EU Climate Resilience Framework aims to strengthen Europe’s ability to anticipate, prepare for, and manage climate risks by getting ahead of those impacts and avoiding damages through coordinated action at EU, national and local levels,” the Commission explained. The Framework combines a proposed European Climate Resilience Law with practical tools and supporting measures to help public authorities, businesses, and other stakeholders make better-informed decisions.” As mentioned, the proposal explains that the European Commission will set up a Climate Insurance Alliance, to help improve insurance protection and support risk prevention across Europe.

Hannover Re raises Acorn Re parametric cat bond target size again, to $450m

Hannover Re has for a second time increased the target size of what will be the seventh catastrophe bond offering in the annual Acorn Re parametric US earthquake series of deals, now seeking $450 million of limit from the Acorn Re Ltd. Hannover Re returned to market with this new and now seventh Acorn Re parametric catastrophe bond earlier this month , initially seeking $200 million of risk transfer and reinsurance limit for the single named ceding insurer, Oak Tree Assurance Ltd. Now, sources have told us the target size has been further increased, with $450 million of parametric earthquake reinsurance limit now being sought.

Mexico gets its first World Bank Cat DDO, to complement cat bonds and parametric insurance

The Government of Mexico has received its first World Bank Catastrophe Deferred Drawdown Option (Cat DDO), which will help the country prepare better and respond more quickly to natural disasters, while also complementing both of its IBRD CAR Mexico 2024 and IBRD CAR Mexico 2024 (Pacific) catastrophe bonds and parametric insurance protection. The World Bank Group has approved US $500 million in financing, marking the first time Mexico has accessed a Catastrophe Deferred Drawdown Option (Cat DDO). We are proud to support the Government of Mexico in this first Cat DDO,” commented Juan Pablo Uribe, World Bank Director for Mexico, Central America, and the Dominican Republic.

Corgi appoints James Kullman as Head of Underwriting, North America

Corgi Insurance, an AI-native insurance carrier built for high-growth companies, has announced the appointment of James Kullman as Head of Underwriting, North America. In his new role, Kullman will lead efforts to advance commercial underwriting across North America, leveraging AI to improve risk selection, develop underwriting guidelines, strengthen portfolio management and support regulatory compliance. He joins Corgi from Chubb, where he served as Assistant Vice President and Pacific North Region Underwriting Manager for the North American Small and Lower Middle Market.
