Buenas Fuentes: Nubank compra banco y Tpaga lanza POS

2. 🤝 M&A

Travelers remains open to M&A following strong Q2

During the company’s July 17 earnings call, Chairman and CEO Alan Schnitzer said Travelers remains interested in strategic acquisitions and would be able to finance an attractive opportunity. Travelers reported $2.2 billion in second-quarter core income, or $10.04 per diluted share, as underwriting gains and higher investment income contributed to a core return on equity of 24.9%. Travelers’ most recent acquisition was cyber insurer Corvus Insurance, purchased for approximately $435 million in November 2023.

3. 🤝 M&A

Verisk Acquires McKenzie Intelligence Services

(Image source: McKenzie Intelligence Services.) Verisk (Jersey City, N.J.) has acquired McKenzie Intelligence Services (London), a geospatial intelligence and event-response company focused on catastrophe and conflict event analysis. MIS provides rapid post-event damage assessment and real-time intelligence for major loss events, according to a Verisk statement. The acquisition brings MIS into Verisk’s Catastrophe and Risk Solutions business, adding geospatial event intelligence to Verisk’s catastrophe models, risk analytics and claims solutions.

4. 💰 Inversión $20M

Terminal raises $20 million

Terminal, which provides a unified API for commercial trucking telematics data, closed a $20 million in Series A financing led by Battery Ventures, with participation from Intact Private Capital, Penske, Y Combinator, and Wayfinder Ventures. The round brings the company’s total funding to $26 million since its founding. Terminal works with insurance, fleet management, and logistics companies.
