Business Moves: Miller Launches Miller Malaysia, Building Regional Footprint; MNK Group Buys Danish Insurer ETU Forsikring

“With Miller Malaysia, we are continuing to demonstrate real momentum as we extend the scope and reach of our market-leading specialist capabilities.” MNK Group Buys Danish Insurer ETU Forsikring MNK Group, the independent, international insurance group, has completed the acquisition of 100% of ETU Forsikring A/S, a Danish non-life insurer authorized since 2006 and headquartered in Rødekro, Southern Jutland, following approval from the Danish Financial Supervisory Authority (Finanstilsynet). MNK Group said its priority is to strengthen ETU as a Danish insurer by committing new capital to the business and growing its existing Danish and Nordic franchise, while continuing to serve ETU’s policyholders and distribution partners. Building on its established presence across the region, Miller Malaysia’s initial offering under its Labuan license will focus on treaty and facultative reinsurance, with plans to broaden its product capabilities over time.

2. 🤝 M&A

Aon Raises $13.5 Billion From Bond Sale to Fund USI Takeover

Aon Plc raised $13.5 billion from a high-grade bond offering to help fund its planned acquisition of USI Insurance Services, marking one of the biggest M&A financing deals to come to market in 2026. Investors placed as much as $65 billion of orders on the sale, with the longest maturity drawing the most demand, according to people familiar with the matter. The $13.5 billion note offering marked the year’s second-largest high-grade bond deal tied to mergers and acquisitions, trailing only Abbott Laboratories’ $20 billion transaction in February.
