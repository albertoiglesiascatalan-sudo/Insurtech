2. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

LA Wildfire Smoke Exposed 7 Million to Dangerous Toxic Metals

A catastrophic 2025 Los Angeles firestorm exposed some 7 million people to high levels of airborne lead and other toxic metals released from burning homes and vehicles, according to a new study. Scientists for the first time were able to quantify in real time the chemical composition of wildfire smoke thanks to a sensor network that went online months before the January 2025 Eaton conflagration that destroyed more than 9,400 buildings, killing 19 people. As climate change triggers urban firestorms like the inferno in the LA suburb of Altadena, the paper published Wednesday in the journal Science Advances offers the first indication of how the incineration of homes, electronics, cars and infrastructure could affect even people who live far from the burn zone due to wind-blown toxic smoke.

3. ⚖️ Regulación

New FCA rules bring ESG rating providers into UK regulation for the first time

The new framework brings ESG rating providers into FCA regulation for the first time, covering a market that plays an important role in investment decisions. New UK rules for environmental, social and governance (ESG) rating providers will raise standards in the market, helping investors to make better informed decisions. ESG rating providers operating in the UK market will need to apply for FCA authorisation from 24 June 2027.

Windward acquires Azimut.ai

Maritime intelligence company Windward has acquired Azimut.ai , adding visual vessel identification technology to its platform. Azimut’s technology uses cameras at ports and maritime chokepoints to identify vessels in real time, including those that switch off their tracking signals to avoid detection. The acquisition marks Windward’s first move into ground- and ship-based sensors.
