2. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Insurance leaders call for collective approach to climate resilience at COP31

At COP31 in Türkiye, the Global Sustainable Insurance Summit examined how the insurance sector can help reduce climate risks, strengthen economic resilience and finance the sustainable transition. Organised by the Insurance Association of Türkiye (TSB) and the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) at the COP31 Insurance House, the Summit brought together executives and specialists from across the re/insurance and resilience sectors. TSB Secretary General Özgür Obalı opened the Summit by stressing that climate resilience is a collective challenge requiring cooperation between the public and private sectors.

Highstreet acquires Players Health retail brokerage

Highstreet Insurance Partners has acquired Players Health’s retail insurance brokerage, establishing a new Sports & Fitness practice. Highstreet and Players Health also plan to expand the relationship between the acquired brokerage and the MGA. Players Health founder and CEO Tyrre Burks will lead Highstreet’s new division beginning December 1, 2026.
