1. 🤝 M&A

Judge Narrows Ben & Jerry’s Suit Accusing Unilever of Silencing Social Activism

A federal judge on Friday dismissed major portions of a Ben & Jerry’s lawsuit accusing its former parent Unilever of trying to silence the ice cream maker’s social activism, dismantle its board and stop funding its namesake foundation. Ben & Jerry’s accused Unilever of violating the 2000 merger agreement by censoring its speech, which has included protests against the war in Gaza, and ousting a chief executive who supported the company’s social activism. District Judge Kevin Castel in Manhattan dismissed seven claims and part of an eighth in a 10-count complaint by Vermont-based Ben & Jerry’s, whose flavors include Cherry Garcia, Half Baked and Phish Food, and by various independent directors.

  1. El dato clave: District Judge Kevin Castel in Manhattan dismissed seven claims and part of an eighth in a 10-count complaint by Vermont-based Ben & Jerry’s, whose flavors include Cherry Garcia, Half Baked and Phish.
  2. ¿Por qué importa? Consolida el mercado y puede redefinir la competencia en el sector.
  3. ¿Qué vigilar? La consolidación reduce actores independientes y abre oportunidades para distribuidores y proveedores de tecnología.

2. ⚖️ Regulación

Sapiens files trademark for SapiensAI

Insurance software provider Sapiens has filed a US trademark application for “SapiensAI.” The August 13 application covers downloadable software, SaaS, and platform services using artificial intelligence across property and casualty, life and annuities, workers’ compensation, medical professional liability, and reinsurance. Beyond software, Sapiens is seeking protection for insurance administration, claims processing and auditing, compliance monitoring, business analytics, consulting, and insurance training services. Sapiens has been leaning further into agentic AI.

  1. El contexto: Beyond software, Sapiens is seeking protection for insurance administration, claims processing and auditing, compliance monitoring, business analytics, consulting, and insurance training services.
  2. ¿Por qué importa? Puede requerir adaptación regulatoria en los próximos meses.
  3. ¿Qué vigilar? Las aseguradoras deberán revisar sus modelos de cumplimiento y posiblemente provisionar costes de adaptación normativa.

3. 💰 Inversión

Exzeo authorizes $25 million share buyback

Exzeo has authorized a program to repurchase up to $25 million of its common stock. The timing and size of any purchases will depend on factors including Exzeo’s share price, available liquidity, and market conditions. The authorization runs for one year and does not require Exzeo to repurchase any specific number of shares.

Cleanup Underway After Tornadoes Touch Down in Delaware and New York’s Long Island

Cleanup efforts and damage assessment were underway Friday in Delaware’s capital and on Long Island, New York, after tornadoes touched down during storms that battered the Northeast with heavy rains and left dozens of people stranded in their cars during flash flooding. Weather service crews were going to view the Delaware and Long Island damage on Friday to determine the strengths and paths of the tornadoes. In other parts of New York, flooding caused part of the Long Island Expressway in Queens to close in both directions, according to New York City Emergency Management.
