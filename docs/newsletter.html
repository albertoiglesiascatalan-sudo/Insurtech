2. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Moody’s RMS says Hurricane Isaias insured losses could hit $4.5bn

Catastrophe risk modeller Moody’s RMS Event Response has estimated total US private market insured losses from Hurricane Isaias to range from $2.5 billion to $4.5 billion, with a best estimate of $3.5 billion. Moody’s said its recent estimate covers insured losses associated with wind, storm surge and precipitation-induced flooding. The figure includes wind and storm surge losses in parts of the Gulf Coast and southeastern US, based on analysis of Moody’s RMS Event Response ensemble footprints in Version 25 of Moody’s RMS North Atlantic Hurricane Models.

  1. El contexto: Moody’s said its recent estimate covers insured losses associated with wind, storm surge and precipitation-induced flooding.
  2. ¿Por qué importa? Impacto directo en exposición, pricing y reservas del sector.
  3. ¿Qué vigilar? El sector deberá revisar modelos de pricing y cobertura en las zonas afectadas ante el incremento de eventos extremos.
