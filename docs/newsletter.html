Insurance companies with established acquisition programmes may be more successful at securing the anticipated benefits of mergers and acquisitions (M&A), according to a joint study by Oxbow Partners, a specialist insurance management consultancy, and InsurTech NY, an organisation supporting connections between insurers, technology businesses and investors. Oxbow Partners and InsurTech NY assessed 333 publicly listed insurance carriers and gathered perspectives from industry executives and transaction specialists with experience of significant insurance deals. Oxbow Partners and InsurTech NY suggest that decisions concerning how businesses will operate together should form part of the initial assessment of a potential acquisition.
