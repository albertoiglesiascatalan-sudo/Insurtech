5. ⚖️ Regulación

Lloyd’s issues guidance and expectations for broker-originated reinsurance structures

As industry focus grows on designing efficient structures to enable capital to connect with reinsurance risk, Lloyd’s has issued guidance on its expectations for any broker-originated reinsurance structures, which is a strategy that Lloyd’s itself believes is likely to see increasing demand. Lloyd’s framework for broker-originated reinsurance structures provides market guidance as to the approach, its expectations and what the approval requirements will be for broker-originated reinsurance structures. “Consistent with Lloyd’s broader approach, broker-originated reinsurance structures are subject to the standard new entrant approval process and ongoing oversight arrangements.”
