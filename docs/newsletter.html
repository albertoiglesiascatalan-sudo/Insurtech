1. 🤝 M&A

Applied Systems acquires InsurTech Tailwind

Applied Systems, a global provider of cloud-based software for the insurance industry, has acquired Tailwind, a document and data retrieval InsurTech company, to accelerate integrated artificial intelligence (AI) automation across the assembly line of insurance. Through Tailwind’s AI-powered technology, Applied aims to further automate the retrieval of carrier documents and their intake into its management systems. As part of Applied, Tailwind will accelerate delivery across the three core domains of the Epic Conductor AI platform: operational AI automation, financial AI automation, and connected AI trading.

2. 🤝 M&A

ILS and alternative capital expanding footprint into DUAE market: AM Best

In a new commentary, rating agency AM Best has highlighted how alternative capital and insurance-linked securities (ILS) are increasingly entering the delegated underwriting authority enterprise (DUAE) space, with DUAEs utilising sidecars, catastrophe bonds, and collateralized reinsurance to secure capacity when insurers step back. In its latest market segment outlook, AM Best has maintained its stable outlook for the DUAE segment, with the agency citing an anticipated moderation in premium growth amid more disciplined expansion efforts. Private equity capital, for example, continues to flow into the segment, driving mergers and acquisitions, where smaller and less-differentiated players are likely to become acquisition targets,” AM Best said.

Specialist Risk Group to acquire Southall Harries

Specialist Risk Group (SRG) agreed to acquire Southall Harries, a commercial insurance broker based in Lichfield, near Birmingham. Founded in 2006 by Matt Southall and Marc Harries, the brokerage specializes in fleet and commercial insurance. The acquisition expands SRG’s UK retail broking operations in the Midlands, adding Southall Harries’ commercial client portfolio and fleet insurance expertise.
