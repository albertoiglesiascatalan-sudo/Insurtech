Hiscox updates return estimates for Syndicate 33 and 6104

Bermuda-headquartered international specialist insurer Hiscox has released current estimates for the 2024 and 2025 accounts of Syndicate 33 and Syndicate 6104, reflecting minor upward revisions across most accounts compared to its August 2026 projections. For the 2024 year of account, the estimates have remained the same for Syndicate 33, standing at 6.1% to 16.1%, on its capacity of £1.696 billion, of which Hiscox holds a 73% share. For the 2025 year of account, Syndicate 33 aims to generate a return of 3.5% to 13.5%, compared to the previous estimated range of 3.4% to 13.4% on its capacity of £1.699 billion, of which Hiscox holds a 73% share.
