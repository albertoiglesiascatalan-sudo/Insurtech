VKB secures upsized €200m King Max Re cat bond renewal priced below guidance

VKB, the German domestic market insurance group, has successfully finalised its latest catastrophe bond sponsorship, securing the upsized target of €200 million of German multi-peril reinsurance protection from the King Max Re II DAC (Series 2026-1) issuance, while the notes have now been priced below the initial guidance, this publication understands. Versicherungskammer Bayern Versicherungsanstalt des oeffentlichen Rechts’ (VKB) ventured back to the catastrophe bond market in October to sponsor its second issuance, with this Series 2026-1 a renewal of sorts for the company’s soon to mature debut cat bond, the €175 million King Max Re 2023 , which matures in early January 2027. We’re now told that the targeted upsizing has been secured, as investor demand helped VKB secure more reinsurance than initially sought, while the pricing was finalised at a level below the initial guidance range indicating strong execution for the cat bond sponsor.

Ceres, a US-based non-profit organisation that leads a national coalition of investors, environmental organisations and other public interest groups working to address global sustainability challenges, has welcomed a new General Counsel opinion letter from the California Department of Insurance clarifying how parametric insurance can be effectively deployed across the state’s admitted market. According to Ceres, the legal opinion is expected to clear a path for wider parametric adoption across California, helping to close protection gaps caused by increasingly frequent extreme weather.
