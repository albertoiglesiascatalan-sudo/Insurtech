2. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

CCRIF to make $17.4m payment to Panama after M7.7 quake triggers parametric coverage

CCRIF SPC, the regional parametric risk pool for the Caribbean and Central America, has announced it is to make a payout of US$17.4 million to the Government of Panama following the magnitude 7.7 earthquake that struck the country on October 9, 2026. “Panama’s decision to secure its CCRIF earthquake coverage through the Fondo de Ahorro de Panamá is a strong example of how parametric insurance can work alongside national savings and stabilization mechanisms as part of a layered approach to financing disaster risk.” Since joining CCRIF in 2018, Panama has received three payouts totalling US$29 million for Hurricane Eta (2020) and 2024 rain events. Using its SPHERA earthquake model, CCRIF estimated peak ground acceleration of up to 0.5 g in Panama, the only CCRIF member country to experience shaking above the model’s 0.03 g reporting threshold.

  1. El contexto: Using its SPHERA earthquake model, CCRIF estimated peak ground acceleration of up to 0.5 g in Panama, the only CCRIF member country to experience shaking above the model’s 0.03 g reporting threshold.
  2. ¿Por qué importa? Impacto directo en exposición, pricing y reservas del sector.
  3. ¿Qué vigilar? El sector deberá revisar modelos de pricing y cobertura en las zonas afectadas ante el incremento de eventos extremos.

3. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Moody’s RMS says Hurricane Isaias insured losses could hit $4.5bn

Catastrophe risk modeller Moody’s RMS Event Response has estimated total US private market insured losses from Hurricane Isaias to range from $2.5 billion to $4.5 billion, with a best estimate of $3.5 billion. Moody’s said its recent estimate covers insured losses associated with wind, storm surge and precipitation-induced flooding. The figure includes wind and storm surge losses in parts of the Gulf Coast and southeastern US, based on analysis of Moody’s RMS Event Response ensemble footprints in Version 25 of Moody’s RMS North Atlantic Hurricane Models.
