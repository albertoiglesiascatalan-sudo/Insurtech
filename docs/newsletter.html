3. 🤝 M&A

Arch to acquire Premier Dealer Services

Arch Insurance North America has agreed to acquire Premier Dealer Holding Corp., including its subsidiary Premier Dealer Services (PDS), expanding its automotive finance and insurance business. Arch said the deal will bring together its specialty insurance expertise with PDS’s administration capabilities, technology infrastructure and dealer relationships. Founded in 1998 and based in Dublin, Ohio, PDS provides automotive protection and loyalty products, administration services, technology and training to dealerships.
