Hannover Re’s seventh catastrophe bond offering in the annual Acorn Re parametric US earthquake series of deals has now been successfully priced at the bottom of initial guidance, to secure a 125% upsized $450 million of reinsurance limit from the Acorn Re Ltd. Hannover Re came back to market with this new and now seventh Acorn Re parametric catastrophe bond earlier this month , initially seeking $200 million of risk transfer and reinsurance limit for the single named ceding insurer, Oak Tree Assurance Ltd. Then, in our second update on the cat bond offering, the target size was further increased, with $450 million of parametric earthquake reinsurance limit being sought and at the same time the price guidance was reduced again.
