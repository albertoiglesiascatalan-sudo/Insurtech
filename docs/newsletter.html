Marrow raises $2 million

Marrow has raised $2 million in pre-seed funding led by Insurtech Gateway, with participation from SEED X Liechtenstein, Leo Capital, Portfolio Ventures and angel investors. Marrow connects insurers’ pricing and underwriting systems to AI agents, allowing insurers, brokers and MGAs to support agent-led insurance shopping. Co-founded by Tim Graham and Adam Mesout, the company originated at Founders Factory.
