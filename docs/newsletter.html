1. 🤝 M&A

Applied Systems acquires InsurTech Tailwind

Applied Systems, a global provider of cloud-based software for the insurance industry, has acquired Tailwind, a document and data retrieval InsurTech company, to accelerate integrated artificial intelligence (AI) automation across the assembly line of insurance. Through Tailwind’s AI-powered technology, Applied aims to further automate the retrieval of carrier documents and their intake into its management systems. As part of Applied, Tailwind will accelerate delivery across the three core domains of the Epic Conductor AI platform: operational AI automation, financial AI automation, and connected AI trading.

  2. ¿Por qué importa? Consolida el mercado y puede redefinir la competencia en el sector.
  3. ¿Qué vigilar? La consolidación reduce actores independientes y abre oportunidades para distribuidores y proveedores de tecnología.

4. 💰 Inversión

Supertake raises $20 million

AI investing startup Supertake has closed a $20 million seed round led by Adjacent, with backing from Union Square Ventures, Coinbase Ventures, Nat Friedman, Nikita Bier, Scott Belsky, Guillermo Rauch, Nemil Dalal, and more. Founded in 2026, Supertake allows people to turn their beliefs into investment portfolios, using frontier AI models and trading agents. Users can connect their brokerage accounts (Coinbase and Robinhood for now) and explain what they believe in, and Supertake’s AI turns it into an investment portfolio.
