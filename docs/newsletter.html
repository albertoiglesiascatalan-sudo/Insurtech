New FCA rules bring ESG rating providers into UK regulation for the first time

The new framework brings ESG rating providers into FCA regulation for the first time, covering a market that plays an important role in investment decisions. New UK rules for environmental, social and governance (ESG) rating providers will raise standards in the market, helping investors to make better informed decisions. ESG rating providers operating in the UK market will need to apply for FCA authorisation from 24 June 2027.
