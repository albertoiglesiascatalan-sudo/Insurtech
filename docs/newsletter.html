1. 🤝 M&A

Inszone acquires Washington employee benefits agency Radwick & Lightle

Inszone Insurance has acquired The Lightle Group, LLC, doing business as Radwick & Lightle, expanding its employee benefits operations in Washington. The acquisition brings Radwick & Lightle’s employee benefits team into Inszone, providing access to a broader range of insurance markets, technology, and operational resources while maintaining the agency’s existing client service model. Founded in 2001 by Curt Lightle and based in Bremerton, Washington, Radwick & Lightle specializes in employee benefits for small and midsize businesses, offering benefits consulting, HR support, compliance services, and claims advocacy.

3. 💰 Inversión

Amwins Global Risks broadens global renewable energy catastrophe facility and raises capacity to $50m

Amwins Global Risks, the specialist wholesale insurance broking business of Amwins, has expanded its specialist natural catastrophe facility for renewable energy projects, increasing available capacity to USD $50 million per placement while extending both the range of covered perils and its global availability. Harry Downes, Director at Amwins Global Risks, commented: “Renewable energy projects are becoming larger, more valuable and increasingly exposed to a broader range of natural catastrophe risks. This facility helps clients close that gap and build greater resilience into their insurance programmes.” Amwins Global Risks added that the enhanced facility is part of its ongoing investment in specialist insurance solutions for the global renewable energy market and is available through the company’s Energy, Power & Infrastructure practice.
