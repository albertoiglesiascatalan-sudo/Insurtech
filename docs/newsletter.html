1. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Munich Re teams with Wawanesa & WWF-Canada to launch parametric river flood solution

Reinsurance firm Munich Re has partnered with Wawanesa and World Wildlife Fund Canada (WWF-Canada), to develop and launch a parametric river flood insurance solution that will deliver rapid funding to safeguard nature-based restoration across the Wolastoq (Saint John River) watershed. Under the parametric structure, Wawanesa funding will be triggered by independently measured river levels, and if predetermined river conditions are met, Wawanesa will provide up to $1 million in funding each year, capped at a maximum of $3 million over a three-year period, to WWF-Canada. The parametric arrangement was developed with support from Munich Re Canada, alongside climate-peril expertise from reinsurance broker Marsh Re.

5. ⚖️ Regulación

Workers’ Compensation Claims Triggered Probes of Walmart for Child Labor Violations

Pennsylvania announced a $7.8 million settlement with Walmart for violations of the state’s Child Labor Act in what the Shapiro Administration said is the largest child labor enforcement action in Pennsylvania history. Investigations were triggered by reports of workers’ compensation claims related to minors. The Bureau of Labor Law Compliance (BLLC) began investigating Walmart in February 2025 after receiving referrals from the Bureau of Workers’ Compensation about minors who were injured while working at Walmart stores.
