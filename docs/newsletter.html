Founded in 2019 and launched in 2021, the San Francisco-based health benefits company offers customizable employer health plans across 47 states.

2. 🤝 M&A

Gallagher acquires Innovise Business Consultants

Gallagher has acquired Englewood, Colorado-based McMillan Insurance & Bonding, which operates as Innovise Business Consultants. The Innovise team, led by Jason McMillan, will relocate to Gallagher’s Denver office and report to Bret VanderVoort, head of Gallagher’s Western Zone retail property and casualty brokerage operations. Innovise provides commercial insurance and surety bonding services, with a focus on the construction, energy, real estate and manufacturing industries.

  1. El dato clave: The Innovise team, led by Jason McMillan, will relocate to Gallagher’s Denver office and report to Bret VanderVoort, head of Gallagher’s Western Zone retail property and casualty brokerage operations.
