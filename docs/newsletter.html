Allianz UK expands hotel and restaurant coverage

Allianz UK has launched a hotels and restaurants offering under its Commercial Select policy for UK businesses with annual turnover between £10 million and £1.5 billion. The offering targets independent restaurants, restaurant chains, hotels and conference centres with hotels. It includes property damage, business interruption, employers’ liability, and public and products liability coverage.

Amwins launches clinical trials insurance product

Amwins has launched an exclusive clinical trials insurance product through its Brokerage division, offering $25 million limits on paper pre-licensed in more than 115 countries. The product was developed with an unnamed Lloyd’s syndicate and is available exclusively through Amwins brokers. Coverage includes clinical trials indemnity, no-fault compensation, clinical testing liability, public and products liability, professional liability and medical malpractice.
