SIAA acquires Canopy Specialty Insurance

SIAA has acquired Canopy Specialty Insurance, a specialty commercial property and casualty MGA operating in the US and UK. The acquisition adds specialty underwriting and product development capabilities alongside SIAA’s agency alliance, retail business Sequel Insurance Agencies and wholesale operation Xchange. Canopy develops and underwrites niche commercial insurance products for small and midsized businesses, including contractors’ liability, excess casualty and legal indemnity programs.

2. 💰 Inversión

Blitzy launches review tool for AI-generated code

Blitzy , an autonomous software development platform backed by Erie Strategic Ventures, Liberty Mutual Strategic Ventures, and New York Life Ventures, has launched PR Review, a feature designed to help engineering teams understand and review AI-generated code faster. The launch addresses a growing bottleneck as AI agents generate code faster than engineers can review it. Blitzy’s process allows engineers to approve the proposed scope and approach before code generation, with automated tests, builds, benchmarks, and static analysis incorporated into development.

  1. El contexto: The launch addresses a growing bottleneck as AI agents generate code faster than engineers can review it.

4. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

PICC P&C appears to have returned for its second Great Wall Re catastrophe bond

Chinese domestic insurer PICC Property and Casualty Company Limited seems to have returned to sponsor its second catastrophe bond using its Hong Kong domiciled special purpose insurer Great Wall Re Limited, as information of a new tranche of notes has appeared albeit with very limited details available at this time. That first Great Wall Re cat bond utilised an indemnity trigger and provided its earthquake reinsurance protection to PICC P&C on a per-occurrence basis and the company called the inaugural cat bond it had sponsored “an important measure to build a dual-pillar risk diversification system of reinsurance and insurance risk securitization.” Now, it appears as if PICC P&C has returned to the cat bond market, with a new tranche of notes issued by Great Wall Re Limited and being featured on certain securities market data services. In that debut instance, PICC P&C sponsored a $32.5 million Great Wall Re Limited cat bond deal, with the notes providing it with a roughly three-year source of fully-collateralized earthquake reinsurance in China, funded by investors from the insurance-linked securities (ILS) market.

El dato clave: In that debut instance, PICC P&C sponsored a $32.5 million Great Wall Re Limited cat bond deal, with the notes providing it with a roughly three-year source of fully-collateralized earthquake reinsurance in China, funded by investors from the insurance-linked securities (ILS) market.
  2. ¿Por qué importa? Impacto directo en exposición, pricing y reservas del sector.
  3. ¿Qué vigilar? El sector deberá revisar modelos de pricing y cobertura en las zonas afectadas ante el incremento de eventos extremos.
