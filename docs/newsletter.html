2. 💰 Inversión

Jamaica’s parametric cat bond payout funded electricity restoration: Chief Fiscal Economist, Francis

After the devastating impacts of hurricane Melissa triggered a full $150 million payout of its IBRD CAR Jamaica 2024 parametric catastrophe bond, the rapid funding from this insurance product helped the country recover its electricity network faster than would have otherwise been possible, the government’s Chief Fiscal Economist explained last week. Speaking at the ILS Bermuda Convergence 2026 conference on the island last week, Terron Francis, Chief Fiscal Economist in the Ministry of Finance & the Public Service, Government of Jamaica highlighted the critical role of risk transfer and insurance risk capital in helping the Caribbean island nation to recover after the impactful storm. Francis said that the parametric catastrophe bond payout arrived within 30 days, supporting response and recovery for Jamaica, without forcing the government into emergency borrowing or substantial programme-budget reallocations.
