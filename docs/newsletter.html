El Niño’s Odds of Being Worst Ever Edge Lower, US Climate Prediction Center Says

The chances that the El Niño spawned by unusually warm seas in the tropical Pacific Ocean will be stronger than the 1982-1983 season have dropped to roughly 60% from a previous forecast of 83%, the US Climate Prediction Center said Thursday. The odds are slipping that the “super” El Niño reshaping global weather patterns will be a record breaker, according to a new outlook from the leading US forecaster. El Niño is expected to fade heading into the Northern Hemisphere summer, with odds of a La Niña outbreak rising to more than 30% during the June-to-August time frame.
