3. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Kin does not expect to tap reinsurance for Hurricane Isaias claims

US-based Kin, a direct-to-consumer provider of home insurance and home financing services, has indicated that claims arising from Hurricane Isaias are unlikely to require support from its reinsurance arrangements, with early loss experience broadly aligning with catastrophe model projections. The insurer anticipates approximately 1,350 claims from the October hurricane, with the majority originating in Florida’s Panhandle region. Isaias struck near Destin, Florida, on 9 October as a Category 2 hurricane, producing sustained winds of 105 mph.

  1. El dato clave: Isaias struck near Destin, Florida, on 9 October as a Category 2 hurricane, producing sustained winds of 105 mph.
