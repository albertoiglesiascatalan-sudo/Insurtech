ILS and alternative capital expanding footprint into DUAE market: AM Best

In a new commentary, rating agency AM Best has highlighted how alternative capital and insurance-linked securities (ILS) are increasingly entering the delegated underwriting authority enterprise (DUAE) space, with DUAEs utilising sidecars, catastrophe bonds, and collateralized reinsurance to secure capacity when insurers step back. In its latest market segment outlook, AM Best has maintained its stable outlook for the DUAE segment, with the agency citing an anticipated moderation in premium growth amid more disciplined expansion efforts. Private equity capital, for example, continues to flow into the segment, driving mergers and acquisitions, where smaller and less-differentiated players are likely to become acquisition targets,” AM Best said.

3. 💰 Inversión

Voltaire discloses $573,000 in funding

Voltaire , the startup using AI to draft claims letters for P&C insurers, disclosed that it raised $573,000 from four investors through simple agreements for future equity (SAFEs). The financing follows a $4.2 million seed round Voltaire announced in May 2025. Founded in 2023 and a team of ~15, Voltaire drafts denial letters, reservation of rights letters and other claims correspondence using language from the applicable policy.
