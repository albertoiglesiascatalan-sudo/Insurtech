Nebuly raises $21 million to measure AI returns

AI analytics startup Nebuly raised a $21 million Series A led by Planven, with participation from Exor Ventures, Tenacity Capital, TGVG Capital, Fastweb+Vodafone via Founders Factory, and returning investors and angels. Based in Turin and New York, Nebuly helps companies measure the business value of AI assistants used by employees and customers. Nebuly connects to existing AI tools, including custom agents and assistants based on Claude, ChatGPT, and Microsoft Copilot, and can run on-premises or in a customer’s private cloud.

Pinion receives approval to acquire Catlin Specialty

Pinion US Holdings has received change-of-control approval from the Oklahoma Department of Insurance to complete its acquisition of Catlin Specialty Insurance Company, a US excess and surplus lines insurer. The approval provides the regulated infrastructure for Pinion’s planned US underwriting operations, which will focus on providing capacity to specialty MGAs. Pinion launched in February 2026 with a commitment of up to $180 million from asset manager Barings.

5. 🌪️ Clima & Catástrofes

Weather disaster losses account for higher share of national income in 13 countries, Munich Re finds

Munich Re research published to coincide with COP 31 in Türkiye has found that weather-related disaster losses accounted for a higher average share of national income in 13 of the 20 countries studied over the past decade than in the first decade of the analysis. The global reinsurer compared losses from weather-related disasters, including floods, storms, hail and wildfires, with gross national income (GNI) in 20 selected countries over 40 years, from 1986 to 2025. Across the 20 countries combined, the share of recorded weather disaster losses that went uninsured fell from roughly two-thirds in 1986-1990 to half in 2021-2025 on average.

  1. El dato clave: Across the 20 countries combined, the share of recorded weather disaster losses that went uninsured fell from roughly two-thirds in 1986-1990 to half in 2021-2025 on average.
  2. ¿Por qué importa? Impacto directo en exposición, pricing y reservas del sector.
  3. ¿Qué vigilar? El sector deberá revisar modelos de pricing y cobertura en las zonas afectadas ante el incremento de eventos extremos.
