Insurance Risks Losing Expertise to Retirements

The insurance industry's persistent talent crunch goes far deeper than a hiring gap – it's also becoming a knowledge gap, as the accumulated judgment of a workforce built over decades retires faster than it can be replaced. Bureau of Labor Statistics estimates roughly 400,000 insurance professionals will have retired in the last half-decade alone. Today's average insurance employee is in their mid-40s to mid-50s, depending on the line of business.
