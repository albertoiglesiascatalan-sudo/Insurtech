1. 🤝 M&A

Assurant acquires Protection Point Service Plans

Assurant has acquired Protection Point Service Plans, a British Columbia-based provider and administrator of commercial equipment protection programs. Protection Point specializes in extended service plans and related protection for equipment used in agriculture, construction, material handling and forestry. The acquisition builds on an existing relationship: Protection Point already worked exclusively with Assurant’s Canadian commercial equipment business, with Assurant underwriting its programs.

Berkshire Hathaway underwriting earnings fall 43% in Q3 as Tokio Marine deal supports P&C reinsurance premiums

Berkshire Hathaway reported a 43% decline in net underwriting earnings across its insurance and reinsurance operations to $1.352 billion for the third quarter of 2026, while earnings for the first nine months fell 16% to $4.8 billion. The Berkshire Hathaway Reinsurance Group delivered pre-tax underwriting earnings of $582 million for Q3’26 and $1.868 billion for 9M’26, compared to $884 million in Q3’25 and $1.227 billion in 9M’25. As in the second quarter, P&C reinsurance top-line was boosted in the third quarter and first nine months of 2026 from the deal with Tokio Marine, under which NICO assumes on a quota share basis a portion of the non-life premiums written and related losses and expenses of the Japanese insurer on risks attaching over a 10-year term.
