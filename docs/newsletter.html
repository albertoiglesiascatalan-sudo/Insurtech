AmTrust International acquires Wakam to strengthen position in UK and Europe

AmTrust International, a specialty insurer and subsidiary of AmTrust Financial Services, has completed its acquisition of Wakam, a business-to-business-to-consumer (B2B2C) insurer serving markets across the UK and Europe. AmTrust said Wakam is now fully capitalised and able to immediately write new business and renew policies across all jurisdictions in its portfolio. The transaction expands AmTrust International’s European operations, adding an established presence in France and broadening its existing UK offering.

5. 💰 Inversión

Symetra and TIAA launch Mariner Re

Symetra and TIAA have launched Mariner Re, a Bermuda-domiciled Class E reinsurer backed by a $750 million equity investment from TIAA. Mariner Re will provide capital to support Symetra’s annuity growth through a flow reinsurance arrangement effective October 1, 2026. Symetra Investment Management and TIAA-owned Nuveen will manage the reinsurer’s investments, combining their insurance asset management and institutional investment capabilities.
